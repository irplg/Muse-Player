Playback rendering references reviewed:
Apple Music-like lyrics (AMLL), @applemusic-like-lyrics/core, AGPL-3.0-only,

https://github.com/amll-dev/applemusic-like-lyrics/blob/9db9236818464ce83f696ee9c5ff0e5395cb744a/packages/core/src/bg-render/mesh-renderer/index.ts

The former maps a filtered album texture onto a mesh. The latter applies two
Pixi BulgePinchFilters to album art. No AMLL source or WebGL presets bundled.

Code actually adapted:
PixiJS Filters, @pixi/filter-bulge-pinch v5.1.1 (MIT)
Git commit d737dd1d7c661823b25741b3fdc0a3045512bebc
https://github.com/pixijs/filters/tree/d737dd1d7c661823b25741b3fdc0a3045512bebc/filters/bulge-pinch
https://registry.npmjs.org/@pixi/filter-bulge-pinch/-/filter-bulge-pinch-5.1.1.tgz
The positive-strength branch of the fragment shader is translated to Kotlin
(bulgeScale). Muse composes two moving centers on bounded Android mesh vertices
rather than sampling two render targets. The album color field, clock and mesh
construction are native Muse code. No Pixi or AMLL runtime is required.
